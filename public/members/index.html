How it works

You make one call. We handle the rest, whether or not the repair ends up as an insurance claim.

  1. Call our hotline or WhatsApp us, any hour

    Call 087 095 5231 or WhatsApp +27 81 849 5489. Tell us you're a member and what's wrong.

  2. We check your membership and policy

    We confirm your membership and that your home insurance is active.

  3. We send a vetted artisan

    Qualified, compliance-checked and contracted to Home Assist.

  4. The artisan diagnoses on site

    If your geyser is still under warranty, we check that first so you don't use up a claim.

  5. We fix it and do the paperwork

    Everyday emergencies are repaired under your labour benefit. Insured events, like a burst geyser, are claimed and managed with your insurer.

Benefit 02Insurance excess cover

When a repair becomes an insurance claim, we pay your policy excess: up to R500 per claim on Essential Cover, and up to R2,000 or 10% of the claim, whichever is lower, on Comprehensive Cover.

Essential and Comprehensive
Benefit 03Full claim management

We manage the whole claim with your insurer: the first plumbing claim, the resultant damage that flows from it, and any building claim, through to repair or a cash settlement. We verify the work and the certificate of compliance, and check every invoice before it is paid.

Comprehensive
Benefit 04Incident verification

Prefer your own service provider? Send us their line-item quote before you claim and we'll advise whether it is in line with fair cost. Once the work is done, we verify that it is safe and compliant, and that everything you were charged for was installed.

Comprehensive
Benefit 05Product warranty management

If your geyser, solar geyser, heat pump or other installed product is still under manufacturer warranty, we manage the warranty claim with the manufacturer instead of using your insurance.
